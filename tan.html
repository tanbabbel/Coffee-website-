Our Story

Born from a Love
of the Bean

We started with a simple belief: every cup of coffee has the potential to be extraordinary. Founded in 2014, BRÛLÉ was born out of an obsession with quality at every step.

We travel the world to find exceptional single-origin beans, building direct relationships with farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and beyond.

🌱
Farm-Direct Sourcing
Direct partnerships with 24 farms across 12 countries ensuring fair pay and exceptional quality.
🔥
Precision Roasting
Small-batch roasting at exact temperature profiles to bring out each bean's unique character.
☕
Expert Baristas
Each team member completes a rigorous 120-hour training program in coffee science and craft.
